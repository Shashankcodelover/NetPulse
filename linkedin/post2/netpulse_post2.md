# NetPulse - Intelligent Network Monitoring

Ever struggled with network blind spots and delayed anomaly detection? Meet the solution: NetPulse. 

NetPulse is an intelligent, AI-powered network monitoring platform designed to provide complete visibility into your infrastructure. In our latest update, we've introduced a unified dashboard that brings all critical metrics into a single pane of glass, alongside Pulse-Bot, our intelligent assistant that helps diagnose issues in real-time. Whether you are on desktop or mobile, NetPulse ensures you have the insights you need to keep your networks running smoothly and securely.
